### Project Overview

**Role:** Head of User Experience  
**Duration:** March 2019 – August 2022  
**Platform:** AI-powered SaaS for e-commerce marketing  
**Stack:** React, Figma, Design Systems, Data Visualisation, Cross-functional Delivery

Machine Labs is an Edinburgh-based startup delivering artificial intelligence–powered marketing and data science solutions to small and medium e-commerce businesses. Its flagship offering enables independent retailers, especially those on Shopify, to harness big-company data capabilities, automate customer engagement, and drive repeat purchases through more effective email and database marketing.

---

### The Challenge: Democratizing Data-Driven Growth

While enterprise e-commerce platforms had embraced advanced machine learning and data-driven growth, these capabilities were largely out of reach for SMEs. Typical pain points included:
- **Fragmented data and tools:** SME teams lacked affordable, unified solutions for targeting, segmentation, and campaign optimization.
- **Opaque insights:** Most marketing platforms failed to surface actionable data, relying on "gut feel" and basic metrics.
- **Need for automation:** Resource-constrained merchants were unable to leverage data science to drive repeat business and retention, or to automate manual workflows. 

The goal for Machine Labs was to bring scalable, enterprise-grade AI marketing to SMEs through Shopify app integration and intuitive, accessible product design.

---

### User Research & Market Insights

- Surveys and interviews with 150+ Shopify merchants uncovered the need for an approachable, trusted platform that could automate complex marketing tasks, personalize customer interactions, and yield real intelligence from messy e-commerce data.
- Beta user pilots revealed demand for clarity, simplicity, and clear value-delivery in both UX and communications.
- The team tracked rapid market adoption, achieving 300 installs across dozens of countries within two weeks of launch, and earning a "Top Pick" feature on the Shopify App Store homepage.

---

### Solution: Product & UX Innovations

#### Unified, Accessible Marketing Suite
- **Shopify integration:** One-click onboarding enabled instant syncing of store data, campaign histories, and customer profiles.
- **No-code automation:** Merchants could deploy and edit automated campaigns, segments, and smart recommendations without technical expertise.
- **Personalized, data-driven campaigns:** ML-driven segmentation and targeting powered relevant, timely communications to the right customer at the right time.

#### Data Science and Insights for Every Merchant
- **Actionable dashboards:** Visualized analytics on customer lifecycle, churn risk, and campaign ROI; embedded guidance and next-action suggestions.
- **AI recommendations:** ML models predicted customer behavior, optimized send times, and surfaced high-value opportunities and at-risk customers.
- **Continuous improvement:** Automatic adaptation to new data, demographic shifts, and seasonality using deep learning models.

#### Design System & UX
- **Consistent design system:** Led end-to-end creation, documentation, and rollout of a reusable design system enabling rapid feature growth and unified product branding (across web and future mobile touchpoints).
- **User-centric workflows:** Streamlined key flows for campaign creation, segmentation, data exploration and performance reporting, validated through iterative prototyping and merchant feedback.
- **Accessibility, clarity and trust:** Focused on language simplicity, microcopy, and inclusive UI for less technical merchant segments.

---

### Business Impact & Recognition

- Platform grew to support thousands of Shopify merchants globally, driving repeat sales and marketing ROI for independent retailers.
- Achieved "Top Pick" on Shopify App Store, immediate global traction and positive merchant reviews for ease of use and time savings.
- Supported rapid sales and VC-led team growth, with multi-million pound funding rounds.
- Created design foundation for extension into multiple sales channels and e-commerce platforms.

---

### Learnings and Recommendations

- **Approachability is a core differentiator:** Even advanced AI must be delivered in a human, actionable, and transparent way for non-expert SMEs to trust and adopt.
- **Design systems unlock cross-team velocity:** Mature design systems were critical to feature velocity, platform scalability, and branding.
- **UX and data-science must be tightly coupled:** Bringing marketers and product teams into early-stage prototyping maximized real-world relevance.
- **Continuous product iteration:** Rapid feedback loops from real merchants kept the tool valuable and differentiated.
